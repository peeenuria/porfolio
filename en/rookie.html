Rookie

A web app for people starting to run or getting back into it, with a tailored plan and a real Decathlon mentor. It's activated when you buy running shoes: scan the barcode and get 3 free months to turn running into a habit, no pressure and no comparing yourself to anyone.

With Camila Palacio and María Camila Portillo Omeara · UX/UI Design Master, Digital Minds 2025/26

In short

  • What I did. Research and interviews as a team; I designed and prototyped my own user stories in code: sign-up, plan and mentor.
  • Result. 5 of 6 interviewees would choose us over Strava, Garmin or a running club.

Starting point

The problem and the solution

The problem

80% of people who buy running shoes quit within six months. They lose motivation because they lack structure, company or a plan made for them. And Decathlon loses a good customer, because people who keep running buy 3 to 5 times more a year.
Figures from the master's brief: a hypothetical scenario, not real Decathlon data.

The solution

A tailored plan and a real Decathlon mentor who stays with you until running becomes a habit. Without the mentor we'd be just another Couch to 5K app, and with pressure we'd be Strava. According to the brief, the goal was for 70% to still be running after six months.

Research

What the interviews told us

We ran 6 interviews, using a landing page as the prototype, with people around us: some wanted to get back into running and some didn't. Three ideas kept coming up.

Alone, they don't keep it up

“When I tried on my own, I couldn't stick with it. What worked for me was running with other people.”

— Interviewee

Impossible goals

“I set myself unreachable goals, like running 20 minutes without stopping. When I didn't make it, I got frustrated and quit.”

— Interviewee

Someone real behind it

“Having a person behind it to guide beginners… nobody does that.”

— Interviewee

Competition

What exists today

Today, anyone who wants to start running has four options: do nothing, Strava, Garmin or a running club. None combines human support with zero pressure.

Strava

Logs your runs, but also compares you.

✕ No support

Couch to 5K

A fixed plan, the same for everyone.

✕ No real mentor

Running club

Real company, at €30–50 a month.

✕ Demands fitness and money

User flow

The main path, from scanning your shoes to your first run: you activate your account, complete your profile, get your plan, meet your mentor and go for a run.

The app

From the shoes to the first run

The journey we designed, in five moments, from scanning your shoes to your first run.

01 / 05

Activate your account

Scan the barcode on your shoes and get three months free. If the camera fails, you type it in by hand.

02 / 05

Your plan, your pace

Tell us what you're after and how much time you have, and the plan starts at your real level, without asking for what you can't do yet.

03 / 05

Meet your mentor

We assign you a mentor based on your profile. You see who they are before you start and book a half-hour video call.

04 / 05

First session

You get to know each other, set goals together and the mentor leaves you a summary of what you agreed.

05 / 05

First run

The session guides you phase by phase. When you finish, you tell your mentor how it felt.

Interface

An inherited brand

The identity was a given: the app is part of Decathlon, so we inherited almost everything from its Design Library. What we did was extend it with the components Rookie needed and the brand didn't have.

Inherited from Decathlon

The base

Brand blue #4C30FF with its 100–1000 scale, Host Grotesk typeface, light and dark modes, spacing and base components: buttons, inputs, modals, pills and icons.

Created for Rookie

Our own

Rookie logo, plan and promo cards, barcode scanner and ticket, profile stepper and weight and height pickers.

Validation

5/6 would choose us over the competition

We validated the concept with 6 interviews and the landing page as a prototype. Each of us scored the hypotheses separately on a scorecard and then we compared notes. The approach won everyone over, even those outside our target audience.

Iteration

What we changed after the interviews

Two changes that came from what people told us.

Blind mentor

See before you start

They wanted to know who their mentor would be before the first session. We assign one based on your profile, but you can browse the catalogue and switch later.

✓ Implemented

Price

Clear pricing

The most repeated question was what happens after the 3 free months. Pricing raised doubts and we left it as an open decision for the next phase.
