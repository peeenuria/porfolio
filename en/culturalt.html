CulturALT

See the prototype ↗

CulturALT brings Valencia's cultural plans together in one place and recommends the ones that suit you: gigs, exhibitions, theatre or comedy, close to your neighbourhood. It explains why it suggests each plan, lets you save it and reminds you so you don't miss it.

With María Paz García Nieto, María Camila Portillo Omeara and Georgiana Lișu · Master's thesis, UX/UI Design Master, Digital Minds 2025/26

In short

  • Problem. Valencia's cultural agenda is scattered across a thousand channels and people find out too late.
  • What I did. Interviews, UX design and the app's illustrations and animations, in a team of four.
  • Result. Coded prototype that scored 93.75 on the SUS across 5 usability tests.

Starting point

The problem and the solution

The problem

Valencia's cultural agenda is spread across Instagram, WhatsApp groups, posters and a pile of websites. Many people find out too late, or never find out at all.

The solution

An app that learns what you like and where you hang out, and recommends plans in Valencia while explaining why they fit you.

Research

What the interviews told us

We talked to people living in Valencia about how they discover things to do. Three ideas kept coming up.

Everything is scattered

“It would be great to have one place where everything is together.”

— Interviewee, 33

They find out too late

“I saw the poster for a mural event in my neighbourhood… three or four months later.”

— Interviewee, 29

They only want their thing

“In the end you get bombarded with ads. If only I could choose what I'm interested in…”

— Interviewee, 33

Competition

What exists today

We looked at where people get their information today, and no option fully solved the problem.

Instagram

You only see what the accounts you follow post.

✕ No reminders

Ticketing platforms

Only big events show up.

✕ No niche events

Listings sites

They have everything, but don't know what you like.

✕ No personalisation

Architecture

User flow

The main journey, from opening the app for the first time to saving a plan: you set your tastes, discover, decide and save it.

The app

Discover new plans

The journey in four moments: set up, discover, decide and don't miss it. Each one answers something people told us in the interviews.

01 / 04

Made to measure

In three steps the app knows what you like and where you go, with no long forms.

02 / 04

Discover

Every plan tells you why we recommend it. That was our answer to the people who told us they only wanted to see their kind of thing.

03 / 04

Decide

Date, price, venue and tickets on one screen, so you can decide without leaving the app.

04 / 04

Don't miss it

Save the plan and the app reminds you, so you never find out three months late again.

My part

Illustration and animation

On top of the interviews and the UX design, I illustrated and animated the empty states to give CulturALT a personality of its own.

Interface

Visual design

A warm, friendly identity: orange as the main colour, with blue and yellow in support, and Manrope so everything reads easily.

Validation

93.75 out of 100 for usability

We ran 5 moderated usability tests with people from our audience. The average SUS score was 93.75, well above the benchmark (68), and almost everyone completed the tasks without help.

Iteration

What we changed after testing

The issues were about clarity: nobody got stuck. These are the three that came up most and how we solved them.

Language · 3 of 5

Language switch

The app started in English because our mentor was English. We added a switch to Spanish.

✓ Implemented

Following a venue · 3 of 5

A notice when you follow

When you followed a venue it wasn't clear what happened. Now a notice confirms the action and explains where to find it.

✓ Implemented

Save or I'm going · 4 of 5

Separate the actions

There were two buttons side by side that seemed to do the same thing. We proposed separating them so each one makes its action clear.
