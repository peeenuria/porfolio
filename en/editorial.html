Editorial illustration

Four commissions for the author Escarlata de Verónica: the first two books of her middle-grade series Alma Alanis and the covers of two poetry collections. For each book I designed the full cover (illustration, typography and ornament) and, for the series, the interior illustrations too.

Client

Escarlata de Verónica

Commission

2 novels + 2 poetry books

Technique

Digital illustration

Year

2024–present

Middle-grade series

Alma Alanis

Two adventure books for young readers. I designed both covers and illustrated the interiors in black and white.

From idea to page

Process

Each interior illustration goes through three stages: a quick sketch to set composition and perspective, inking with a textured line and, finally, the halftone screen that adds volume and ties the whole series together visually.

also by the same author

Poetry collections

Covers for two poetry collections published on Amazon KDP: «De niña buena a madre loba» and «Era de villana». Both explore womanhood and motherhood through the author's intimate voice.

Also in publishing · Imaginaria Books

The Art of War

I illustrated Sun Tzu's classic following the black-and-white, ink-wash and halftone style of Alma Alanis. The challenge: turning abstract strategy concepts into scenes full of tension and movement.
