Comic

La Lotería

My bachelor's thesis: the first chapter of a comic about amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). Two young siblings, both diagnosed, try to run a campaign to raise awareness and funds while learning to accept their diagnosis. The title comes from a line by Montxo Iriarte, president of ANELA, about the randomness of the disease: «Its causes are unknown. It comes, it appears, just like that. And you've won the lottery».

Before drawing

From topic to page

Before drawing, I did research: I gathered testimonies from people close to the disease, contacted associations and studied how other works had handled similar topics. With that I defined the problem, the goals and the work plan.

The problem

— A disease little known among the public — Fragmented healthcare — Insufficient or late social support — It also affects families and carers — Little research funding

The goals

— Explain what ALS is — Make patients' difficulties visible — Point out how the authorities handle the disease — And a personal one: a tribute to my uncle

The plan

— Research and testimonies — Genre study: coming-of-age and realism — Script and storyboard — Character and set design — Pencil, ink and colour

Interiors

Pages and characters

Rhythm and silence: wordless sequences let the landscape breathe between scenes.
